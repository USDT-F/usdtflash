When I profit, how does withdrawal work?

What happens if I lose? Do I lose the entire capital?

No, you only lose the amount you risked on that specific trade, not the entire capital. The direct deposit system gives you full control over risk management:

Example: Balance: 500 USDT-Flash → Trade with 50 USDT-Flash → Loss → Remaining: 450 USDT-Flash

Each package is linked to one platform of your choice at purchase. If you want to use multiple platforms, you can purchase multiple packages (one per platform). To switch platforms, simply complete your trades, withdraw your balance, and purchase a new package with your desired platform. Full flexibility to match your needs.
