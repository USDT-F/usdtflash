Frequently Asked

How is the winner selected?
The winning ticket is selected using a verifiable on-chain random number generator. The contract hash and selection transaction are published publicly for anyone to audit.
What happens if not all tickets are sold by Dec 31?
The lottery will still proceed on the deadline date regardless of how many tickets have been sold. The full 1 BTC prize is guaranteed to be awarded.
How do I receive my Bitcoin if I win?
The 1 BTC prize is transferred directly to the USDT-FLASH wallet associated with your winning ticket. No additional steps required.
