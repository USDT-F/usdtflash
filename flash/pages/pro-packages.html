If a position hits the drawdown limit or loses, the contract auto-reverts. The firm's pool absorbs the loss. Your funded account resets. Try again — no penalty.
06

Consistently profitable? Upgrade to a higher tier. Want to exit? Settle all positions, return the capital to the contract, and keep your accumulated profits. No lock-in period.

Rules you can understand

No fine print, no hidden clauses. Here is every rule that applies to your funded account — in plain language.

How to qualify
  • 1. Pay the one-time challenge fee for your market package. That is the only payment — ever.
  • 2. Trade normally. There are no minimum trading days and no profit targets to hit.
  • 3. As long as you stay within the drawdown limit, your account stays funded. No expiry.
Max drawdown (the limit)
  • • Binary Options: 10% — you can lose up to $1,500 of the firm's capital before the contract pauses.
  • • Betting: 8% — up to $4,000 in losses allowed.
  • • Forex: 5% — up to $7,500 in losses allowed.
  • • Hit the limit? The contract freezes the account and returns remaining capital to the firm pool. You can restart with a new challenge fee at any time.
Profit split (how you get paid)
  • • You keep 80% of every profit. The firm keeps 20%.
  • • Payouts are processed automatically every 14 days. No request needed — the smart contract sends your USDT.
  • • No minimum withdrawal. Even $5 in profit gets split and sent to your wallet.
  • • If you lose, you pay nothing. The firm absorbs the loss. No negative balance, no debt.
What happens if you breach
  • • If your account hits the max drawdown limit, the smart contract automatically pauses all trading and reclaims remaining capital.
  • • There is no "rebuild" phase or extra fees. You can restart with a new challenge fee immediately.
  • • You are not banned. Restart as many times as you want. Many traders restart after a bad week.
Scaling up (get more capital)
  • • Consistently profitable? The firm automatically increases your capital. No extra fees or re-evaluation.
  • • Binary → Betting → Forex: you can move up as your track record grows. Higher capital, same rules.
  • • Want to stay on your current tier? No pressure — you keep trading indefinitely with the capital you have.
