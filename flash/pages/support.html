Frequently asked questions

What packages do you offer and what are the prices?
Trial Package — $36 for 500 USDT FLASH, delivered in 2-5 minutes. Basic Package — $120 for 4,500 USDT. Pro Package — $149 for 3,500 USDT (our most popular, includes bonus 500 USDT and VIP support). Enterprise Package — $399 for 10,000 USDT, instant delivery, personal account manager. Every package comes with a 100% money-back guarantee.
How long does delivery take after payment?
Trial and Basic packages: 2-5 minutes. Pro package: 1-3 minutes. Enterprise package: instant. If you haven't received your USDT FLASH after 30 minutes, contact us immediately. Delays are rare but can happen during blockchain network congestion.
Which platforms can I use with USDT FLASH?
Betting: Stake, 1xBet, BC.Game, Duelbits, Rollbit. Binary Options: Pocket Option, Quotex, IQ Option, Olymp Trade, Deriv. Forex: MetaTrader 4/5, cTrader, Exness, IC Markets, Pepperstone. Each package is linked to one platform at purchase. To use multiple platforms, purchase a package for each.
How do I pay and which methods are accepted?
We accept USDT (TRC20) via Binance, Bybit, OKX, Bitget, RedotPay, MetaMask, and Trust Wallet. All payments go through the TRON blockchain. Make sure you're using the TRC-20 network when sending.
Can I get a refund if something goes wrong?
Yes. We offer a 100% money-back guarantee. Refund requests must be submitted within 48 hours. Eligible cases include non-delivery after 24 hours, technical errors on our side, or duplicate transactions. Contact support with your transaction ID and details. Processing takes 1-3 business days.
How do I keep my account secure?
Enable two-factor authentication (2FA), use a strong password, and never share your credentials. If you notice suspicious activity, change your password immediately and contact our security team. We monitor accounts 24/7 for unusual activity.
